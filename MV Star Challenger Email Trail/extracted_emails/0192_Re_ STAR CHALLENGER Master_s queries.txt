        GENERAL

 INFORMATION ON PORT QASIM AND ITS EXISTING FACILITIES / RESTRICTIONS:

  - Country           : Pakistan

  - Port Name      : Port Mohammad Bin Qasim

  - Co-ordinates  : 24.46 N, 67.21 E

  - Local Time      : GMT + 5 hours

  Port Muhammad Bin Qasim is located 35 KM away from city center of Karachi. This is the second largest port of Pakistan after

 Karachi.

  The port is relatively a newer port and thus has a number of dedicated terminals/ wharves for various cargoes. It handles a

 wide range of general, bulk, bagged, container and liquid cargoes.

  FYI, Port Qasim is the only Pakistani port handling coal shipments with different dedicated terminals, where night time navigation

 is restricted and all berthing / sailing conducted during day light.

   - Pilotage is compulsory and minimum two tugs are required  for every movement.

   - Generally shipping movements take place during daylight hours and are totally controlled/regulated by port authority.

   - Night navigation facility (subject to additional charges & approval from authorities) Can be extended to the ships with max

 LOA of 310 M fitted with operational GPS and Bow Thruster.

   - Saturday and Sunday are weekly holidays at Port Qasim offices.

   - Cargo operations at Port Qasim are 24h/7d, except during strikes/force major or in case of any restriction from vessel/receivers/terminal.

   - Free Pratique is considered granted upon vessel's arrival Pilot Boarding Ground. However, Health Clearance is issued by authorities

 during ship's port stay.

   - Specific Gravity at Port Qasim is 1.022-1.025

   - All Ports in Pakistan are ISPS compliant and all security conditions are strictly observed.

   - Present Security level being maintained at 1 [ONE].

  -  All Flag Vessels are permitted to call Pakistani ports except Israel, Taiwan, India, Iran and DPRK (North Korea).

    PORT QASIM ELECTRIC POWER COMPANY TERMINAL:

  The terminal is adjacent to the power plant and is fully equipped with shore facility to hand bulk coal with the quickest possible

 turnaround. There are two shore cranes fitted with own grabs used for discharging cargo from holds and moved over the conveyor belt to the storage area.

  The daily discharge rate is around 20,000/22,000 MT PWWD whereby a vessel with a load of 50,000/55,000 MT completes cargo operation

 in about 2 to 2.5 days.

  The present parameters / restrictions in force at the terminal are:

  BEAM                             :         33.00 M

  DRAFT                            :         12.00 M

  MAX LADEN DWT        :     75,000.00 MT

  AIRDRAFT                      :         No restriction

   Please note that the use of Open Loop Scrubber is not permitted in Pakistan. Vessel must switch to either Closed Loop Scrubber

 or compliant fuel (< 0.5% sulphur) well before entering Pakistani territortial waters. Attached govt. circular to this effect.
